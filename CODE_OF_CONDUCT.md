# Code of conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project free from harassment and to
respect people of every background, identity, experience, and ability.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, specific, and evidence-led.
- Critique ideas and changes, not people.
- Welcome questions and make technical context accessible.
- Protect private reports, credentials, and personal information.
- Accept responsibility, correct mistakes, and preserve an accurate record.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, sexualized attention, deliberate
intimidation, doxxing, and publication of private information are not accepted.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository maintainers. Maintainers
will review the available evidence, protect confidentiality where possible, and
apply a proportionate response. Retaliation against a reporter is unacceptable.

This project may remove, edit, or reject contributions and may temporarily or
permanently restrict participation when behavior violates this code.
